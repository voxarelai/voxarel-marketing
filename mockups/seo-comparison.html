Switching to Voxarel, answered.

Is Voxarel a full CargoWise replacement?

For regional cargo and courier operators, yes: Voxarel covers bookings, warehouse, finance, cash, tracking and field operations in one system. For global multi-modal forwarders with deep EDI needs, CargoWise may fit better.

How long does it take to move over?

Most operators are live in days to a few weeks. We help import your rates, branches and open shipments so you start with real data.

What does Voxarel do that CargoWise does not?

Native cash-on-delivery reconciliation, WhatsApp notifications, a built-in AI assistant, and deep focus on the Gulf–India corridor.
