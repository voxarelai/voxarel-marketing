Dubai to Chennai shipping, answered.

How long does cargo take from Dubai to Chennai?

By sea, LCL cargo typically takes 7–12 days door to door; by air, 1–3 days. Customs clearance at Chennai adds time when paperwork is incomplete, which is why Voxarel tracks every document against the shipment.

What documents are needed to ship from Dubai to India?

Usually a commercial invoice, packing list, airway bill or bill of lading, and any commodity-specific certificates. Voxarel keeps each document attached to the shipment and flags what is missing before dispatch.

Can I collect cash on delivery in India?

Yes. Voxarel collects and reconciles cash on delivery in INR at the destination, settled per driver and branch, and matched back to your AED accounts.

Does Voxarel handle consolidation on this lane?

Yes. Consolidate many shippers into one Dubai–Chennai movement, build the container manifest, and split costs and cash back to each customer automatically.
